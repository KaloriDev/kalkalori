## Bulk-Mean transport derivation

Existing local heat/mass correlations and the uniform-source approximation are
retained. Let `g=1/(Rf+Rd)`, `b=Rf/(Rf+Rd)`, `a=1-b`, and `L=Q_latent`.
`Rf` is the gas-film resistance; `Rd` includes the other film, wall and fouling.
Eliminating the interface from the two resistance balances gives, per unit
normalized area, `qs=g*(Tg-Tc)-a*L` and `qt=qs+L=g*(Tg-Tc)+b*L`.
Gas sensible cooling satisfies `Ch*dTg/dx=-qs`. Other-stream heating satisfies
`Cc*dTc/dx=qt` in cocurrent flow and `Cc*dTc/dx=-qt` in counterflow.
Each stream's own inlet temperature is its boundary condition.

The previous arithmetic terminal means instead implied
`Q=g*DeltaTin/[1+g/2*(1/Ch+1/Cc)]` at zero condensate, a different transport
law from the dry epsilon-NTU closure. Conservation alone cannot repair it.

`uniform_source_bulk_means` analytically integrates the linear energy
equations, with no axial segments. Define `E(z)=expm1(z)/z`,
`J(z)=(E(z)-1)/z`, `M(z)=(J(z)-1/2)/z`. Taylor sums supply their removable
singularities; counterflow integrates from the end giving a decaying
exponential, avoiding overflow at high NTU and cancellation at equal capacities.

For cocurrent flow, `k=-g*(1/Ch+1/Cc)` and `f=L*(a/Ch-b/Cc)`:
`mean(Tg-Tc)=DeltaTin*E(k)+f*J(k)`; `Q=g*mean(Tg-Tc)+b*L`.
The mean weighted temperature follows from
`Ch*mean(Tg)+Cc*mean(Tc)=Ch*Tg,in+Cc*Tc,in+L/2`.

For counterflow, `k=g*(1/Cc-1/Ch)`, `f=L*(a/Ch+b/Cc)`.
When `k<=0`, `Q=[g*E(k)*DeltaTin+g*f*J(k)+b*L]/[1+g*E(k)/Cc]`.
When `k>0`, integrate backwards:
`Q=[g*E(-k)*(DeltaTin+L/Ch)-g*f*J(-k)+b*L]/[1+g*E(-k)/Ch]`.
Integrating the gas equation once more gives its area mean; the other mean
then follows from `mean(Tg-Tc)=(Q-b*L)/g`.

For outside-mixed crossflow, integrate each unmixed inside-stream path first.
With the gas outside, set `A=E(-g/Cc)`, `K=g*A/Ch`, and
`S=(1-b*A)*L/(g*A)`. Then
`mean(Tg)=Tc,in+DeltaTin*E(-K)+S*(1-E(-K))` and
`mean(Tc)=Tc,in*A+(mean(Tg)+b*L/g)*(1-A)`.
With gas inside, exchange which stream is mixed and integrate its sensible
equation; no anonymous min/max-capacity substitution changes physical sides.
At `L=0` these equations recover both existing outside-mixed crossflow branches.
N-section phase-change rejection remains in force.

The analytic kernel passes 72 dry-limit tests across three topologies, both
physical sides, equal/unequal capacities, and NTU 1e-5 to 100, plus 24
independent numerical boundary-value ODE comparisons. These checks establish
the constant-property linear transport mathematics, not experimental accuracy
or coupled nonlinear fin applicability. Coupled validation remains in progress.

The Bulk-Mean coupled solver uses these transport means in the existing
interface balance while retaining its property/HTC bulk-evaluation convention.
The latent source is iterated rather than fitted. A mean is no longer required
to equal the midpoint of terminal bounds; convergence checks its transport
source closure instead. The linear wet-area envelope remains a separate 0D
approximation. Nonlinear radial fin behavior still needs independent validation.

## Initial numerical and performance evidence

Fresh exact-base inverse profile: original default test failed at 300.015 s,
six forward evaluations, 36 property iterations, nine joint evaluations,
161 region solves, 1678 region iterations and 24371 profile points.
Full pytest runtime 307.11 s under cProfile; other baseline probes ran
concurrently, so this is diagnostic timing, not an isolated speed comparison.
Raw profile: `.pytest_cache/v098-inverse-before.pstats`; XML/log alongside.
The profile attributes ~176 s to radial response and ~90 s to gas enthalpy
evaluation. No deadline, tolerance or skip/xfail changed.

Outside condensation previously inverted a full unrelaxed enthalpy/humidity
trial before relaxing its temperature. This can reject a valid fixed point
before damping takes effect. Conserved-variable relaxation is now applied
before inversion, following the established inside-solver strategy.
Further phase-domain validation is in progress.
