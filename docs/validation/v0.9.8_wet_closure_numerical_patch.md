## Bulk-Mean transport derivation

Existing local heat/mass correlations and the uniform-source approximation are
retained. Let `g=1/(Rf+Rd)`, `b=Rf/(Rf+Rd)`, `a=1-b`, and `L=Q_latent`.
`Rf` is the gas-film resistance; `Rd` includes the other film, wall and fouling.
Eliminating the interface from the two resistance balances gives, per unit
normalized area, `qs=g*(Tg-Tc)-a*L` and `qt=qs+L=g*(Tg-Tc)+b*L`.
Gas sensible cooling satisfies `Ch*dTg/dx=-qs`. Other-stream heating satisfies
`Cc*dTc/dx=qt` in cocurrent flow and `Cc*dTc/dx=-qt` in counterflow.
Each stream's own inlet temperature is its boundary condition. This changes
the mean transport closure, without changing the local correlation or fitting
any fixture duty. The uniform source remains a 0D approximation.

The previous arithmetic terminal means instead implied
`Q=g*DeltaTin/[1+g/2*(1/Ch+1/Cc)]` at zero condensate, a different transport
law from the dry epsilon-NTU closure. Conservation alone cannot repair it.

`uniform_source_bulk_means` analytically integrates the linear energy
equations, with no axial segments. Define `E(z)=expm1(z)/z`,
`J(z)=(E(z)-1)/z`, `M(z)=(J(z)-1/2)/z`. Taylor sums supply their removable
singularities; counterflow integrates from the end giving a decaying
exponential, avoiding overflow at high NTU and cancellation at equal capacities.

For cocurrent flow, `k=-g*(1/Ch+1/Cc)` and `f=L*(a/Ch-b/Cc)`:
`mean(Tg-Tc)=DeltaTin*E(k)+f*J(k)`; `Q=g*mean(Tg-Tc)+b*L`.
The mean weighted temperature follows from
`Ch*mean(Tg)+Cc*mean(Tc)=Ch*Tg,in+Cc*Tc,in+L/2`.

For counterflow, `k=g*(1/Cc-1/Ch)`, `f=L*(a/Ch+b/Cc)`.
When `k<=0`, `Q=[g*E(k)*DeltaTin+g*f*J(k)+b*L]/[1+g*E(k)/Cc]`.
When `k>0`, integrate backwards:
`Q=[g*E(-k)*(DeltaTin+L/Ch)-g*f*J(-k)+b*L]/[1+g*E(-k)/Ch]`.
Integrating the gas equation once more gives its area mean; the other mean
then follows from `mean(Tg-Tc)=(Q-b*L)/g`.

For outside-mixed crossflow, integrate each unmixed inside-stream path first.
With the gas outside, set `A=E(-g/Cc)`, `K=g*A/Ch`, and
`S=(1-b*A)*L/(g*A)`. Then
`mean(Tg)=Tc,in+DeltaTin*E(-K)+S*(1-E(-K))` and
`mean(Tc)=Tc,in*A+(mean(Tg)+b*L/g)*(1-A)`.
With gas inside, exchange which stream is mixed and integrate its sensible
equation; no anonymous min/max-capacity substitution changes physical sides.
At `L=0` these equations recover both existing outside-mixed crossflow branches.
N-section phase-change rejection remains in force.

The analytic kernel passes 72 dry-limit tests across three topologies, both
physical sides, equal/unequal capacities, and NTU 1e-5 to 100, plus 24
independent numerical boundary-value ODE comparisons. These checks establish
the constant-property linear transport mathematics, not experimental accuracy
or experimental accuracy of nonlinear fin predictions. A further independent
nested crossflow integration checks both physical sides; reversed heating and
equal inlet tests cover signed sensible transfer. The kernel has 101 tests.

The Bulk-Mean coupled solver uses these transport means in the existing
interface balance while retaining its property/HTC bulk-evaluation convention.
The latent source is iterated rather than fitted. A mean is no longer required
to equal the midpoint of terminal bounds; convergence checks its transport
source closure instead. The linear wet-area envelope remains a separate 0D
approximation. Nonlinear radial fin behavior retains its native equations.

## Common caloric and wall reference

Transport integration alone did not eliminate the coupled discontinuity:
the old dry path advected whole-mixture cp while the wet path conserved dry
carrier enthalpy plus IF97 vapor enthalpy and drained IF97 liquid. v0.9.8
uses finite stream enthalpy increments for both explicitly selected Bulk-Mean
paths, e.g. `Cg=md*[h(Tin,W)-h(Tout,W)]/(Tin-Tout)`. A one-sided caloric
derivative supplies the equal-temperature limit. The coolant uses its native
finite enthalpy increment, including the existing integral adapter for sensible
providers without native enthalpy. Correlation cp and the Chilton-Colburn mass
transfer coefficient remain unchanged. Dry-only temperatures outside the
saturated-vapor caloric domain retain their original sensible calculation.

The generic thermal functions accept a private optional capacity callback;
it is inactive for other providers. Installed conductance remains unchanged;
the existing process-area reserve scales duty. Dry Bulk-Mean wall-property
iteration uses the same analytic area-mean wall states as its zero-source wet
limit, with separate metal and exposed fouling boundaries.

The inherited fin cold-zone fallback expressed its equilibrium offset relative
to an arithmetic terminal mean. Changing to transport means requires a change
of reference. With `Rd` downstream and `Rf` gas-film resistance, subtract
`[(Tg,transport-Tg,arithmetic)*Rd +
(Tc,transport-Tc,arithmetic)*Rf]/(Rf+Rd)` from that offset. This is obtained
directly by eliminating the interface in the resistance balance. It is not
a metal-temperature clamp or an empirical duty correction. The historical
endpoint-finned regression remains admissible and passes.

Twelve strict coupled zero-condensate comparisons cover bare/circular fins,
counter/cocurrent/outside-mixed crossflow, clean/fouled surfaces and 10% thermal
reserve. Bare duty agreement is within 3e-7 relative or .001 W; the independent
radial-FVM versus analytic dry-fin comparison retains its discretization
allowance of 5e-5 relative. These are tighter diagnostic controls, not changes
to production defaults. The constitutive limit is exact at fixed properties.

## Bounded phase states and nonlinear closure

Gas inversion bounds are the inlet temperature and the maximum of coolant
inlet, water triple point and dew point at the trial outlet humidity. Caloric
evaluation never widens a bracket above the inlet or retries an arbitrary
backend failure. Endpoint monotonicity, finite inputs and intermediate mapping
are checked; exhausted bisection is an error rather than a returned root.
`WetGasEnthalpyError` preserves ValueError compatibility and carries a reason
and optional diagnostics. Reasons distinguish invalid input, bad bracket,
non-monotonic mapping, backend failure, physical bounds and numerical stagnation;
operation timeout retains the existing budget exception.

Both outlet humidity and enthalpy are relaxed before inversion. Inadmissible
steps are backtracked; native coolant enthalpy inversion also preserves the
liquid phase and terminal bounds. An outside fixed point that stagnates uses
a bounded residual solve of the same energy, vapor, latent-source and wall
equations. Its humidity coordinate is a fraction of
`min(Win,Wsat(Tgas,out))`; it cannot accept supersaturation. Its final energy
gates are at most .01 W and never looser than the configured physical gates.
The solver reuses exact property snapshots, not approximate property values.
Optimizer stagnation is not classified as proof of global physical infeasibility.

The old large-area inside regression exposed a separate invalid historical
success: at 308.635863 K it returned Wout=.05823505 while Wsat=.03778687.
The new shared inversion cannot accept this state. Conserved-variable
backtracking ends with a classified numerical-stagnation error and a diagnostic
state, rather than widening into supersaturated vapor. Its regression now
requires rejection; a valid inside case independently verifies saturation.
The outside-only Bulk-Mean provider's applicability has not been extended to
inside condensation. No claim is made that the physical exchanger itself is
impossible; its old mean-model prediction was inadmissible.

## Literature and scope

Recovered local audits covering Osakabe, Fujii, Kanzaka, Nuntaphan, Cheng and
Juhola were reviewed for phase, drainage and limiting behavior. Their geometry,
gas composition, wetting and boundary differences prevent treating them as a
paired predictive validation dataset for this patch. In particular, wavy or
continuous plate fins do not establish a spiral-fin pressure-drop law.
No empirical wet HTC, wet fin-efficiency or wet hydraulic correlation was
introduced. Condensate film resistance, retention, carryover, re-evaporation,
row/circuit segmentation and private VDI J2 remain outside this patch.

Provider identities, default Elmahdy selection, outside-only Bulk-Mean
Simulation applicability, N-section rejection and existing hydraulic reference
warnings remain. Private results retain authoritative achieved duty,
`Q_process_kW`, and condensate semantics; no notebook reorganization or release
version/CHANGELOG operation is part of this work.
