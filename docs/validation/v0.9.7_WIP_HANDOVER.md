probe. Rejected alternatives: invented row/pass connections, clamping means,

saturation, latent heat and drain enthalpy in the two condensation solvers.
Chosen solution (UNVERIFIED): topology-compatible linear interface balances
using the current latent source and mean resistances. Rejected alternatives:
diagnostic-only correction and broad segmented wet redesign. Validation pending.

empirical reduced HTC includes unresolved contact. Chosen solution: report the
mean-efficiency surface mean; leave fin base/tip and extrema unavailable.
