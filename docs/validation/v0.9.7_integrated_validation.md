wall/interface temperatures within the existing 0D models. The review found no
new blocking wall-temperature defect in the examined dry, near-onset, partial
and fully wet calculations. The direction of the Bulk-Mean changes follows

## Temperatures actually used in the equations

### Bulk-Mean Heat/Mass Transfer Analogy

The active path is `BulkMeanAnalogyWetCoilProvider.simulate` →
`legacy_wet_coil_integration._apply_legacy_outside` →
`outside_condensation_solver.solve_outside_condensation`. The retained internal
filename is historical; no old public class alias was reintroduced.

| Operation | Actual physical selection and equation |
| --- | --- |
| Initial onset | `integration.evaluate_side_onset` uses the dry **minimum exposed skin** and inlet-composition dew point. The unchanged .5 K band makes active onset require a margin greater than .25 K at zero extra onset tolerance. Possibility and activation remain separate. |
| Iterative wet area | Current mean-composition dew point is compared with the representative **interface** envelope. Counterflow pairs coolant inlet/gas outlet and coolant outlet/gas inlet; cocurrent pairs inlet/inlet and outlet/outlet. Unresolved crossflow retains the opposite-stream mean, not a Cartesian area distribution. |
| Representative interface | Each bare representative state closes `(Tg−Ts)/Rf + Qlatent = (Ts−Tc)/Rd`, with current mean-property film resistance `Rf=1/(h*A)` and downstream inside-film, fouling and wall resistance `Rd`. The same representative full-area latent source is held uniform; these samples are not separate local full-area heat loads. |
| Fraction and wet temperature | `wet_surface_fraction.estimate_wet_surface_fraction` assumes a linear distribution: `f=(Tdew−Tsmin)/(Tsmax−Tsmin)` within its physical bounds; `Twet=(Tsmin+min(Tdew,Tsmax))/2`. A uniform-envelope fallback retains its existing activation ramp. `Awet=f*A`. |
| Saturation and mass | `condensation_solver_helpers.solve_condensing_interface_state` evaluates `Wsat(p,Twet)`. `mass_heat_transfer.condensation_rate` uses `h/cp * Le^(−2/3) * max(Wmean−Wsat,0) * Awet`, capped at the available water. The physical dry HTC is used, not the latent-inclusive equivalent HTC. |
| Latent and sensible | `Qlatent=mcond*hfg(Twet)`. `Qsensible=h*A*(Tgmean−Tsglobal)` uses the entire sensible area. The global interface satisfies `Qsensible+Qlatent=(Tsglobal−Tcmean)/Rd`; a metal-wall diagnostic is not substituted for it. |
| Drain and achieved duty | `condensate_enthalpy_flow` uses `Hdrain=mcond*hl(Twet)` when condensate exceeds the unchanged mass threshold. Gas closure is `mdry*(hin−hout)=Q+Hdrain`; `Wout=Win−mcond/mdry`. Coolant outlet uses the existing mean-cp closure. Independent finite-enthalpy increments check that approximation separately. |

The representative envelope enters the actual area, equilibrium and mass/heat
calculations. The native wet reporting envelope is subsequently reconstructed
from accepted wet states; it does not substitute a dry solve at wet outlets.
With fouling, exposed surface, inner/outer metal and bulk fluid remain distinct
resistance nodes. Circular fins retain their nonlinear radial primary/fin
sources, geometric areas and existing optional cold-zone equilibrium offset.
That offset is not a measured metal temperature. Wet continuous plate fins
remain unsupported; no unavailable local tip field is invented.

The important approximation is uniform mean latent loading and a linear
two-point surface distribution, with one mean composition. A real local wet
fraction and local vapor depletion need a spatial model. Increasing the span
does not prove that its complete distribution is experimentally correct.

### Elmahdy–Mitalas

`wet_coil_integration.forward_wet_process` constructs the provider's own
thermodynamics and inside/wall adapter, then `wet_coil.solve_coil` solves its
native dry/wet source profile. Its dry cold-end interface and inlet dew point
decide onset. For partial wetting, the native wet/dry boundary is solved where
the interface reaches the dew point. The Bulk-Mean fraction is never imposed.

The wet-region saturation-enthalpy secant is built from native interface
temperatures. The moisture source solution uses an **effective saturation
temperature** derived from that source-profile solution, including vapor
enthalpy ratios; it is not the generic arithmetic wall mean. Local drainage
uses the native primary interface temperature `Ts(z)`, or the native radial
mass-weighted liquid enthalpy callback for a finned surface. The solver checks
`Qgas=Qliquid+Hdrain`, endpoint water removal against integrated source density,
positive wet driving force and admissible gas humidity. Its integral duty is
the achieved coolant duty.

The public `W_sat_wet_surface` calculated at a reported wet mean is a diagnostic;
it does not replace the source-profile saturation law. Similarly, new native
surface/profile means are reporting integrals. Bare source-profile calculations
do not call Bulk-Mean's explicit `mcond*hfg(Twet)` as their governing duty law.
The radial adapter does integrate mass, latent heat and drainage at its own
local surface temperatures. These formulations are deliberately preserved.
The relevant kernel, thermodynamic adapters and radial code remain identical
to the merged base; focused source/profile and onset tests passed.

## The historical dry/wet duty discontinuity

Bulk-Mean dry dispatch uses epsilon-NTU; its active sensible balance uses
arithmetic bulk means. Even with `mcond=0`, those are different closures.
With constant coefficients and zero latent heat the latter gives
`Q=UA*ΔTin/[1+UA/2*(1/Cgas+1/Cwater)]`, which generally differs from counterflow
epsilon-NTU. The unchanged activation band can switch between them at a finite
positive subcooling margin. Full gas/water conservation does not remove that
constitutive difference.

| .01820 | .252167 | 4762.343 / 0 | 4765.294 / .000001478 |
| .01821 | .260997 | 4762.386 / 0 | 4765.558 / .000001583 |

At W=.01820, the same-boundary disabled result is approximately 4751.982 W.
The base's ~10.36 W difference exists without latent heat. The current result
adds 3.613 W latent with the accompanying sensible response; the observed
13.36 W increment across adjacent W samples is not a pure latent discontinuity.
Elmahdy follows its own continuous native onset branch in this range; its
internal dry closure also need not equal the generic disabled dry calculation.

TERMOTECH demonstrates why the limitation matters in engineering use. At the
nominal 32 °C water inlet, base active zero-condensate Q is 245.0163 kW against
226.5086 kW disabled: an **8.17% pre-existing closure difference**. At 34 °C,
base AUTO is 239.6062 kW with zero condensate, current AUTO 239.8985 kW with
.000129953 kg/s and .314329 kW latent, and disabled is 221.4820 kW on both
snapshots. At 34.25 °C, AUTO returns the disabled 220.8532 kW on both. This
large switching behavior is present before v0.9.7; the wall correction neither
creates nor repairs its main component.

**Disposition:** separately scoped historical model limitation, not a new
v0.9.7 wall-temperature acceptance blocker. It remains material for onset
sizing and high-effectiveness exchangers. A justified fix must reconcile the
sensible/wet process formulation and activation treatment, then verify the
enthalpy domain and conservation. Forcing continuity, reverting positive
condensate, raising tolerances or multiplying Q would not be justified here.

cold exposed surface and mean/metal/interface quantities distinct; it does not

0–.003228. The sign and increasing driving force with colder surface are
