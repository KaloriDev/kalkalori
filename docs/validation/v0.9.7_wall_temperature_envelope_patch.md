## Initial architecture findings (before implementation)

* Dry Rating and Simulation call `thermal_iteration.estimate_wall_temperature_envelope`.
  It recalculates local HTCs and resistance balances for the Cartesian product of
  fluid terminal temperatures, irrespective of thermal topology. Countercurrent
  and cocurrent terminal pairs are therefore mixed with physically incompatible
  pairs. Its mean is the midpoint of the extrema, not a mean-state calculation.
* The converged `IterativeThermalState` remains the authoritative global dry
  property/resistance state. Its resistance-balanced mean heat rate is distinct
  from the epsilon-NTU achieved duty. Neither quantity should be rescaled to make
  a temperature envelope fit. Rating terminal states can differ from the
  achievable Simulation terminal states used by its mean-property iteration.
* `outside_dispatch` owns film, wall, fouling, root and fin/contact branches.
  Circular fins already have an annular temperature-ratio model. Primary, base
  and tip nodes must be reconstructed with the signed branch heat rates, and a
  fin's area mean follows its efficiency, not the base/tip midpoint.
* Continuous plate fins use the experimental Wang/Schmidt dry network. Schmidt
  supplies a mean efficiency and no tip temperature ratio. Empirical contact is
  included in the reduced HTC; an intrinsic film/contact split or a radial tip
  temperature cannot be reconstructed from these data.
* `integration.evaluate_side_onset` screens the cold exposed outside skin, but
  falls back to the metal node when a skin is unavailable; its reported mean
  can mix a core-metal definition with exposed-skin bounds. Bare tubes with
  outside fouling currently report the metal node as the exposed skin.
* Legacy's `outside_condensation_solver` has a second, physics-active envelope:
  gas inlet/outlet against a fixed coolant mean, shifted to the previous global
  interface mean. This envelope sets mass-transfer area, saturation and drain
  enthalpy. The analogous inside solver repeats the construction. Correcting
  only public reporting would leave these equations inconsistent.
* Legacy circular fins own a nonlinear radial solution and an existing cold-zone
  0D fallback; these cannot be replaced by a dry radial profile. Elmahdy owns a
  native wet/dry source profile and radial response, with axial quadrature means.
  It must retain these independent model definitions. VDI is external/private.
* `wet_surface_fraction` estimates a linear 0D temperature-distribution area;
  it does not resolve an axial length. Elmahdy's axial fraction and fin radial
  area fraction have different definitions. Provider applicability remains owned
  by `wet_coil_provider`; continuous sheets remain unsupported for wet operation.
* Private `.kon/_shared/case_study_reporting.py` extracts core walls and exposed
  extrema into the thermal table. It lacks a whole exposed-surface mean and
  explicit interface/provenance handling. `ProcessState.q_process_w` already
  maps achieved process duty without an additional area factor and must remain
  unchanged. Excel exports consume the same centralized tables.
