## Accepted numerical and experimental limits

The wall-state correction changes some Bulk-Mean numerical results while
retaining Elmahdy regression results. This does not establish experimentally
proven improvement. The historical dry epsilon-NTU versus active arithmetic-
mean sensible closure switch remained a separate limitation.

Matched independent experimental predictive validation remains incomplete.
Osakabe supports local onset/wetting trends, conditional Mihara cases have
geometry/humidity limitations and historical Bulk-Mean domain failures, and
no reproducible paired Liang duty/condensate run was recovered. Neither
supplier fixtures nor local trend agreement establish predictive accuracy.

The next proposed patch, v0.9.8, combines the historical wet/dry closure,
high-NTU enthalpy/phase-domain and inverse-Rating performance investigations.
They are not delivered here. Broad repository/private-workflow cleanup remains
deferred until just before v1.0.0.
