0 → .002439754 kg/s; IPROS118 approximately 155.592 → 169.625 kW.

geometry/humidity limitations and historical Bulk-Mean domain failures, and

supplier fixtures nor local trend agreement establish predictive accuracy.

high-NTU enthalpy/phase-domain and inverse-Rating performance investigations.

deferred until just before v1.0.0.
