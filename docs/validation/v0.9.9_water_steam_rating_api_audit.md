## Finding and correction

**MISSING EXPECTED CAPABILITY, corrected:** public water-evaporation Rating
rejected `inside.m_dot=None` even when an independent hot-side duty and an
explicit outlet state uniquely determined the water flow. Condensation
already supported its corresponding enthalpy-drop closure. New public tests
reproduced the rejection on the merged base before implementation.

The evaporation adapter now resolves:

```text
delta_h = h_out_target - h_in > 0
m_dot_water = Q_required / delta_h > 0
```

Duty comes from positive explicit `Q`, a fully specified outside temperature
program, or both if consistent. The outside program retains the established
`m_dot_outside * cp(mean_temperature) * (T_in - T_out)` convention. An outlet
without a known inside mass flow cannot independently supply duty. Missing
outlet, missing independent duty, unknown outside flow, nonpositive enthalpy
rise and nonfinite/nonpositive duty or derived flow are rejected.

A separate internal `BalanceSideSpec` uses the original canonical inlet
(`T+p`, `p+h` or `p+x`) and one canonical `h_out`. This reuses the condenser's
existing reconstruction helper, renamed to cover both processes. The
resolved input supplies the existing evaporator solver, closed balance,
diagnostics, public result construction and optional Simulation. The caller's
frozen input is unchanged. Known-flow evaporation retains its prior duty
resolution and solver path. Condensation retains its prior flow resolution
and saturated-liquid default.

No heat-transfer/friction correlations, zone equations, iteration tolerances,
hydraulic models or supported physical regimes were changed. The only routing
extension sends an underdetermined unknown-flow water-heating request to the
water adapter so it receives the explicit missing-outlet diagnostic.

## Public result and conservation contract

`closed_balance.inside.m_dot` and
`inside_phase_change.mass_flow_total` expose the calculated water flow.
`mass_flux` uses that flow divided by the installed tube flow area per pass.
Each non-empty zone uses the same flow for its duty and HTC. Public checks
cover:

- `Q_required = m_dot_water * (h_out - h_in)` and matching outside energy;
- `Q_total = Q_preheat + Q_evaporation + Q_superheat`;
- `A_required = sum(A_zone)` and `UA_required = sum(UA_zone)`;
- known-flow/calculated-flow equality of HTC, zone heat flux, areas, U, UA,
  outlet temperatures and public exchanger diagnostics;
- total water mass conservation; `m_dot_evaporated` is the liquid-to-vapor
  conversion rate, not an additional mass outlet, and `m_dot_condensate=0`;
- intentionally unavailable two-phase dp and warning codes;
- optional Simulation receiving the same resolved flow without changing the
  caller's `m_dot=None` or canonical inlet specification.

Two-phase states do not acquire fictitious single-phase transport values.
For an isothermal phase-changing inside stream, the closed balance's
`cp_mean` and `C` may be `None`. Multizone equivalent capacity/HTC and the
wall envelope retain their existing 0D reporting meanings. Rebuilding a
saturated Simulation inlet must use `p+h` or `p+x`, not its ambiguous `T+p`.
